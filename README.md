# mba-machine-learning-project
Data-Driven Credit Risk Optimization for Retail Lending Institutions
Executive Summary:
Zenith FinBank is a mid-sized retail banking organization that operates in India, providing personal, education, auto, and small business loans. However, over the past few years, the bank has been experiencing a steady increase in the number of loan defaults, resulting in higher Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) and lower profitability. The current credit risk assessment process is highly dependent on traditional credit scoring and manual credit assessment, making it difficult for the bank to proactively identify high-risk borrowers.
To mitigate this issue, Zenith FinBank intends to use machine learning approaches to create a predictive model that can predict the probability of a loan default at the time of loan application. By using past loan data with demographic, financial, and behavioral characteristics of the borrowers, the bank intends to enhance credit risk assessment, minimize defaults, and improve overall portfolio quality. The final goal is to enable informed lending decisions that strike a balance between growth and risk management.

Problem Statement:
Can we effectively forecast whether a loan applicant has a high chance of defaulting on the loan based on demographic, financial, and loan-related variables that are available at the point of loan application?
In other words, the bank would like to develop a binary classification model that helps to identify high-risk loan applicants in advance so that risk can be mitigated proactively.

Business Domain and Context:
Zenith FinBank is engaged in the retail lending business, providing various types of loan products such as personal loans, car loans, education loans, and small business loans. The bank handles a large number of loan applications on a monthly basis and competes fiercely in a growing but cautious financial environment.
Over the past few years, the bank has noticed an increase in the number of loan defaults, which has affected the bank's profitability and resulted in an increase in Non-Performing Assets (NPAs). Loan defaults not only result in a reduction in interest income but also result in increased costs of recovery, legal charges, and capital provisioning. Achieving a balanced approach between loan growth and credit risk management has thus become a key strategic focus.
At present, the bank uses conventional credit evaluation processes that involve credit score checks, income verification, debt-to-income ratio analysis, and manual credit evaluation. Although these processes enable the bank to make a preliminary assessment of the credit risk, they do not make optimal use of the extensive historical data available. Additionally, manual credit evaluation can be time-consuming, unstandardized, and prone to human error.
By integrating machine learning into the loan approval process, the bank aims to:
•	Identify high-risk applicants before loan disbursement
•	Improve the accuracy and consistency of credit risk evaluation
•	Optimize interest rate pricing based on risk levels
•	Reduce default rates while maintaining healthy loan growth


A predictive model would serve as a decision-support tool for credit officers, enabling data-driven lending strategies and improved portfolio performance.
